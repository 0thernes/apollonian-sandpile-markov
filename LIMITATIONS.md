# Limitations & Falsification Criteria (`LIMITATIONS.md`, v3.0.0)

## Scope Boundaries

1. **Mostly known results.** All 16 statements (`T1a`–`T3e` plus `T2f`) are kernel-checked, and most formalize known results: HKRS (Ann. of Math. 2024) for three packings (`T1c`), Lagarias–Mallows–Wilks (2002) general `d`-dimensional Soddy integrality (`T1e`), Dhar (1990) Abelian sandpile confluence and universal sinked termination (`T2a`), Markov (1879/1880) descent + Zhang (Acta Arith. 2007) mod-32 theorem (`T3b`), Hurwitz (1907) complete `n = 4` classification and Vieta orbits (`T3e`), Fricke and Cohn (1955) (`T3a`), and classical reciprocity (`T1b`, `T3c`). The only statement with no prior statement found is `T2d`, an elementary parity lemma. The project does not prove the Apollonian local-global classification, the `STAT-058` scaling limit, or the Markov uniqueness conjecture (`NT-083`), and no theorem is a bounded case of these problems.
2. **Remaining declarations narrower than their descriptive names.** Release `v3.0.0` closed four previous scope gaps (`T1e` general `d`-dim Soddy, `T2a` universal sinked termination, `T3b` Markov descent for all positive solutions, and `T3e` complete Hurwitz `n=4` Vieta orbits). The following declarations remain explicitly narrower than broader literature theorems (names retained for comparator continuity):
   - `T1d` is mod-8 residue locking on one octahedral orbit plus five standalone closed Jacobi evaluations. Nothing is shown about squares at `b3`–`b6` or twice-squares at `b1, b2, b4, b5`, and no global octahedral reciprocity argument is formalized. Reciprocity obstructions for generalized/octahedral packings were given in arXiv:2510.21702 (Shi, Shi, Whitehead, Williams-Tracy, Zhang, 2025).
   - `T2b` and `T2f` prove the direct 4×4 and 9×9 Laplace determinants (`192`, `100,352`, `1,092`, `5,393,920`), block similarities, and explicit legal Dhar traces, but no Matrix–Tree theorem or abstract critical-group-identity theorem is formalized.
   - `T2c` covers one isotropic quadratic, not the Levine–Pegden–Smart classification.
   - `T2d` requires the Laplacian bound `K` to equal the mean curvature `(4A - 2B + 4C)/M`; it says nothing about height-5 sandpile configurations or the `A_2` sandpile identity.
   - `T2e` is an edge-normal closure identity for lattice polygons and a `2×2` determinant identity; the Kalinin–Shkolnikov theorem is not formalized.
   - `T3d` follows one mutation path of depth five. Lean states positivity of values at positive integers; positivity of Laurent coefficients holds by inspection of the subtraction-free definitions but is not a stated theorem. All-depth positivity (Lee–Schiffler 2015) is not formalized.
3. **Contrast packings.** `rootSquareContrast = (-4,8,9,9)` is only shown in Lean to have Descartes form 0 and contain the square `9`. HKRS list quadratic (`3n^2`, `6n^2`) and quartic (`n^4`, `4n^4`) obstructions for it, so it is not an unobstructed packing. Mod-24 residue-set comparisons are computational, not Lean statements.
4. **Finite cutoffs in computational audits.** BFS orbit counts (`≤ 1000`, `≤ 1500`), Markov tree enumeration (`≤ 10^7`), and Hurwitz `n=4` tables (`≤ 120`) are finite checks. They corroborate the Lean statements on finite ranges and do not replace proofs.
5. **Kernel replay coverage.** In Ubuntu CI, `leanchecker --fresh` and `leanchecker-paranoid` (`lean4lean`) replay the `Solution` and `PalomarSolution` modules; `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, and `con-ron` check an export of the three root declarations (`prove_all_apollonian_sandpile_markov_theorems`, `NonVacuity.nonvac_all_hypotheses`, `Adversarial.all_adversarial_refutations`) and their dependency closure; `lake comparator --paranoid` compares all 17 targets in `lean/comparator.json` with `PalomarChallenge.lean`; and a live negative rejection gate tests tampered `sorry`, custom `axiom`, and statement-alteration inputs.
6. **Comparator stubs.** `lean/PalomarChallenge.lean` contains 17 intentional `by sorry` stubs (the challenge side of the comparator and not a build target). All built modules contain zero `sorry`.
7. **Local C11 execution vs. CI.** On Windows hosts without GCC/Clang on PATH, the ISO C11 verifier, 100k fuzzer, and `libFuzzer` target run in Ubuntu GitHub Actions CI under `-fsanitize=address,undefined`; Rust and Python run on both Ubuntu and Windows.
8. **Review status.** Internal hardening cycles and the 2026-11-26 statement audit (whose 13 paper items and 9 code items are all resolved in `v2.0.0`, see [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md)) are logged openly. Formal external journal peer review remains pending.

## What Would Prove Us Wrong (Concrete Falsification Criteria)

Any of the following reproducible findings would falsify a claim and would be recorded in `ERRATA.md`:

1. **Kernel / axiom failure:** any of the six kernels run in CI (`lean 4.35.0`, `leanchecker --fresh`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) rejecting the checked modules or export, or `lake comparator --paranoid` reporting a statement mismatch or an axiom beyond `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
2. **HKRS / octahedral counterexample:** an integer quadruple reachable from `(-3,5,8,8)`, `(-7,17,12,20)` or `(-11,21,24,28)` containing a curvature `m^2`, or an octahedral sextuple reachable from `(-6,10,17,40,24,17)` breaking the mod-8 pattern `(2,2,1,0,0,1)`.
3. **A2 odd-Laplacian counterexample:** an integer function `u : ℤ² → ℤ`, `M ≥ 1`, quadratic form `(A,B,C,D,E)` and doubly periodic `ψ` with `Δ_{A2} u ≤ 5` and `5M = 4A - 2B + 4C`.
4. **Even Markov mod-32 violation:** a Markov triple generated from `(1,1,1)` with an even entry `x ≢ 2 (mod 32)`, or an odd prime divisor `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`.
5. **Cluster identity / Hurwitz failure:** integers `(x1,x2,x3)` with `P1 * P4 ≠ x1^4 x2^2 x3^2 P2^2 + P3^2` or `P2 * P5 ≠ x1^6 x2^4 x3^2 P3^2 + P4^2`, or a positive integer solution of the `n=4` Hurwitz equation with `a ∈ {2,3}` or `a ≥ 5`.
6. **Differential / memory-safety failure:** a discrepancy between Rust, ISO C11, Python, and Lean outputs on shared inputs, or an ASan/UBSan violation in `native/asm_verify.c`, `native/fuzz_asm.c`, or `native/fuzz_libfuzzer.c`.
