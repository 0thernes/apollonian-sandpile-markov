# Statement Fidelity Audit (`docs/statement-fidelity.md`)

Each headline theorem in `paper/main.tex` is listed below with its Lean 4.35.0 proposition in `lean/Challenge.lean` and `lean/PalomarChallenge.lean`. Corrected on 2026-11-26 after an independent statement audit: where the paper or the declaration name says more than the Lean proposition, the third column says what Lean states (see `ERRATA.md`). `NonVacuity.lean` gives witnesses for theorem premises but not a joint witness for every theorem (none for the `T2d` hypothesis bundle or `IsStabilizingOdometer`).
