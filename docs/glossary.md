# Glossary (`docs/glossary.md`)

- **Abelian Sandpile (Chip-Firing Model):** A deterministic discrete dynamical system on a finite sinked graph where any vertex with chips $s(v) \ge \deg(v)$ topples by sending one chip to each neighbor (and losing boundary edges to the sink). By `T2a`, stabilization is confluent (independent of firing order).
- **Apollonian Circle Packing:** An infinite fractal packing of mutually tangent circles generated by reflecting integer curvature quadruples $(a,b,c,d)$ across the Descartes quadratic form $Q_{\mathrm{Desc}} = (a+b+c+d)^2 - 2(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2) = 0$.
- **Bareiss Algorithm:** Exact fraction-free integer Gaussian elimination for computing matrix determinants without floating-point rounding or rational blowup.
- **Cohn Matrix Tree:** The infinite binary tree of pairs $(A, B) \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})^2$ with $\operatorname{tr}(ABA^{-1}B^{-1}) = -2$ generated by Nielsen moves from Cohn's root pair, whose divided traces $(\operatorname{tr}A/3, \operatorname{tr}B/3, \operatorname{tr}(AB)/3)$ parametrize all Markov triples (`T3a`).
- **Dhar Critical Identity:** The unique idempotent recurrent configuration $e = \operatorname{stab}(2\mu_{\max} - \operatorname{stab}(2\mu_{\max}))$ forming the identity element of the sandpile critical group $\mathcal{K}(G)$ (`T2b`, `T2f`).
- **Even-Laplacian Rigidity ($\mathbb{A}_2$):** Our original theorem (`T2d`) proving that any doubly periodic integer superharmonic envelope on the 6-regular triangular lattice $\mathbb{A}_2$ with mean Laplacian $K$ must have even $K \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, universally obstructing $K = 5$.
- **HKRS Obstruction:** The global quadratic-reciprocity obstruction discovered by Haag, Kertzer, Rickards, and Stange (*Annals of Mathematics*, 2024) forbidding all perfect-square curvatures in certain primitive integral Apollonian packings despite local mod-24 admissibility (`T1c`).
- **Markov Quiver Cluster Algebra:** The skew-symmetric Fomin–Zelevinsky cluster algebra on three vertices with double arrows $1 \rightrightarrows 2 \rightrightarrows 3 \rightrightarrows 1$, whose exchange relations produce positive integer Laurent polynomials specializing to Markov numbers at $(1,1,1)$ (`T3d`).
- **Octahedral Packing ($\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-2})$):** A thin Lorentzian reflection orbit of curvature sextuples $(b_1,\dots,b_6)$ satisfying antipodal sum invariance and $(b_1-b_4)^2+(b_2-b_5)^2+(b_3-b_6)^2 = 2S^2$, governed by arithmetic in $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-2}]$ (`T1d`).
- **Palomar Comparator:** Contract verification tool (`lake comparator --paranoid`) confirming that a solution module proves the exact literal theorem statements of a challenge contract with zero `sorry` and zero unpermitted axioms.
- **Soddy–Gosset Equation:** The $d$-dimensional mutual tangency equation $d\sum_{i=1}^{d+2} b_i^2 = (\sum_{i=1}^{d+2} b_i)^2$, whose Vieta reflection multiplier $\alpha_d = 2/(d-1)$ is integral iff $d \in \{2, 3\}$ (`T1e`).
- **Tropical Curve Balancing:** The zero-sum condition $\sum_i w_i v_i = (0,0)$ satisfied by outgoing primitive edge directions around every vertex and outer polygon of a sandpile relaxation tropical curve (`T2e`).
- **Vieta Involution:** The quadratic root-flipping reflection $x_i \mapsto L_i(x_{-i}) - x_i$ unifying Pillars I, II, and III.
