# Claims Matrix (`CLAIMS.md`, v3.0.0)

Each claim in `apollonian-sandpile-markov` (`0TH-2026-018`) is mapped below to its Lean declaration, its exact scope as stated in Lean, and its relation to prior work. Releases `v2.0.0`–`v3.0.0` resolve all 13 paper/doc items and all 9 formal-code items from the 2026-11-26 independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md); see also [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md)), close four major formal gaps in `v3.0.0` (`T1e` general `d`-dim Soddy dichotomy, `T2a` universal sinked termination & existence, `T3b` Markov's descent theorem for all positive solutions, and `T3e` complete Hurwitz `n = 4` Vieta-orbit bi-implications), expand `Adversarial.lean` to 10 refutations, and exhaustively axiom-audit **884 declarations** via `VerifyAllDecls.lean`.

Overall: most statements are zero-Mathlib formalizations of known results; the rest are finite computations, elementary identities, and one elementary parity lemma (`T2d`) with no prior statement found. No statement is a bounded case of `STAT-058` or `NT-083`.

## Tier 2 — Computation (Rust, ISO C11, Python; not Lean)

- `C1`: BFS orbit statistics (`results/rust_summary.json`, `results/exact_verification_report.json`) for the 4 Apollonian roots (curvatures `≤ 1000`), 2 octahedral roots (`≤ 1500` / `≤ 400`) and the 3D Soddy root (`≤ 150`), with Rust and Python outputs compared in CI. The mod-24 residue sets quoted for `T1c` and `T1d` come from these computations.
- `C2`: Exact 15×15 and 65×65 Dhar identity computations on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $\mathbb{A}_2$ (`results/figure_stats.json`), plus Rust/C11/Python Bareiss determinants independently confirming Lean's `T2b` and `T2f` (`192`, `100,352`, `1,092`, `5,393,920`).
- `C3`: Markov tree enumeration up to `10^7` (`56` triples, `56` distinct numbers, no uniqueness violation in this range, `61` odd primes `p ≡ 1 (4)`, even residues mod 64 `{2, 34}`) and a Hurwitz `n=4` search up to `120`. Finite ranges only.
